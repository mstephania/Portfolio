About Me


Hi! I'm Mindy, a Bioinformatician (Ph.D.) and Data Scientist based in Chile.

My work sits at the intersection of biology, data, and technology. With over 10 years of experience in multi-omics and plant transcriptomics, I specialize in building reproducible pipelines and translating complex genomic data into actionable insights for sustainable agriculture and precision science. Recently, I've expanded my toolkit into Machine Learning, Python, and digital tools to bridge the gap between molecular research and data-driven decision-making.

When I'm not writing code or analyzing RNA-Seq data, you'll find me exploring new ways to apply AI to real-world environmental and biological challenges.
